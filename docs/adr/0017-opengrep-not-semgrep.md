# SAST is Opengrep, not Semgrep

The SAST Engine is Opengrep with Opengrep rules (LGPL-2.1), not Semgrep CE plus official Semgrep rules. Semgrep-maintained rules are not OSI and forbid competing products. We rejected "official Semgrep rules, internal-only" and "permissive third-party rules only." Gitleaks (MIT) and Trivy (Apache-2.0) are unchanged.
